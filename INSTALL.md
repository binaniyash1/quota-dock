# Install Quota Dock (2 minutes)

## What this is

A small app that floats on your screen and shows how much AI quota you have left.

**Not a website. Not localhost. Not npm.**

---

## Step 1 — Download

Get the installer for your computer from **GitHub Releases**:

- **Mac**: `Quota-Dock_0.1.0_aarch64.dmg` (Apple Silicon) or `_x64.dmg` (Intel)
- **Windows**: `Quota-Dock_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe`

## Step 2 — Install

- **Mac**: Open the `.dmg`, drag Quota Dock to Applications, open it.
- **Windows**: Run the `.exe`, follow the wizard.

## Step 3 — Connect (one time)

1. A floating widget appears on your screen.
2. Click **Auto-detect logins**.
3. If something is missing, click **Open login** for that service, sign in, then Auto-detect again.

Done. Drag it anywhere. It stays on top of every app.

---

## Controls

- **Move**: drag the top bar
- **Hide**: click `–` (find it in your menu bar / system tray)
- **Show again**: click the tray icon
- **Pin**: click 📌

---

## Troubleshooting

**"Black screen on localhost"** — ignore localhost. That was a dev prototype. Use the installed app.

**"Unidentified developer" (Mac)** — Right-click → Open → Open.

**Provider not connecting** — Make sure you're logged into that AI tool on the same computer, then click Auto-detect again.
